Our story

The idea of Happy Kind was conceived when Joey bought a painting for her son, with a caption saying “fill the world with kindness”.

In this modern world, kindness has taken a backseat to self-interest in fueling happiness. At Happy Kind, we firmly believe that Happiness and Kindness should go hand in hand in making the world a better place.

So who we are? We are no different than the other mommies and kiddos’ stores, we sell to make a living. Just that, we make a difference by Doing Good.
